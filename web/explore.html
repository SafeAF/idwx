Context for every comparison.

ERA5/Oikolab is historical reanalysis at the Twin Falls coordinate, not a physical observing station. It is separate from KTWF observations and live forecast guidance on the homepage.

Daily gaps stay missing. Partial periods are labeled and excluded from full-period aggregates. Historical P10–P90 describes the archive; it is not a forecast interval. Freeze-date distributions include confirmed events only; no-event and incomplete seasons are counted separately.
