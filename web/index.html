A note from the field

History offers context.
It doesn’t promise a date.

The planning-year projection uses the historical P50 as its central date and P10–P90 as its historical range. Spring P90 is a later last-freeze date; fall P10 is an earlier first-freeze date. This is a climatology baseline, not a model forecast or a probability guarantee.
